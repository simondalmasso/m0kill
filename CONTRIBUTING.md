# Contributing to m0kill

m0kill is an **evidence-first research repository**, not a finished product or an automated earning service. Contributions should improve accuracy, reproducibility, safety, or the decision process—not merely add features.

## Before proposing work

1. Read [AUD_CANON.md](./AUD_CANON.md) (decision authority) and [ARQ_CANON.md](./ARQ_CANON.md) (one-ARQ operating contract).
2. Search [existing issues](https://github.com/simondalmasso/m0kill/issues). Closed competition lanes are evidence, not a request to restart them.
3. Propose the smallest testable change with a source link, exact claimed benefit, cost, acceptance gate, and a way to disprove it.
4. Use a dedicated branch and pull request. Do not force-push `main`, rewrite historical branches, or merge your own unreviewed experiment.

## Evidence checklist

For factual or empirical claims include, as applicable: official rules and UTC dates; account/geo/automation eligibility; payout net of fees; legal zero-owner-spend path; pinned revisions and artifact hashes; exact commands and outputs; frozen seeds, paired baselines and raw trials; official-engine evidence; submission IDs and readbacks.

Label unknowns **UNKNOWN**, unproven prototypes **UNPROVEN**, and blocked work **BLOCKED_WITH_EVIDENCE**. Do not advertise hypothetical earnings as paid earnings.

## Safety and secrets

No credentials, keys, account tokens, private wallet data, paid execution, KYC bypass, CAPTCHA bypass, unauthorized access, or market/competition-rule circumvention. Sensitive vulnerabilities should not be posted with exploit details in public issues; contact the maintainer using an appropriate private channel.

## Licensing

A project-wide open-source license is **not yet specified**. Check ownership and third-party licenses before proposing copied material. Do not assume repository visibility grants reuse rights.
